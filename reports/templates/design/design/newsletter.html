This Month at a Glance

The April programme month closed with the highest fistula surgical throughput in 12 months — 23 operated across the four UNFPA-supported camp sites, against a monthly target of 25. Cumulative operated cases for CP10 now stand at 41 of the 100-case annual target, keeping the campaign on a credible trajectory to close out the country programme cycle near plan.

MPDSR review activity rebounded sharply this month after a March dip: 38 events reviewed with 14 closed as IMPLEMENTED and 9 at FUNDED awaiting facility-level rollout. Eleven events remain PENDING — four of them in Sunamganj for more than 30 days, the largest single-district backlog of the year.

Operational Signal

Cox's Bazar referral-to-admission gap is widening — and the explanation isn't routing.

Camp-based fistula identification continues to track well — CIPRB outreach teams logged 842 beneficiaries across Teknaf and Ukhia in April, the highest district volume in the programme — but only 58% of referred cases reached admission, down from 71% in March. Field interviews and case-level review point to two drivers: persistent surgical-bed congestion at Cox's Bazar District Hospital and, increasingly, family-side delay in cases requiring travel beyond the camp perimeter.

A structural read: throughput at the district-hospital end is the binding constraint, not field identification. Continued investment in screening without parallel investment in operative capacity is unlikely to close the gap.

Without bed-side and theatre-side capacity in Cox's Bazar matching the pace of camp screening, every additional identified case becomes an additional referral the system cannot honour within an acceptable window. — Programme M&E Note, 01 May 2026

National Context

The national Maternal Mortality Ratio reported by SVRS for 2023 is 136 per 100,000 live births, down from a 2019 baseline of 165 — meaningful progress, but still nearly double the SDG 3.1 target of 70. The skilled-birth-attendance indicator continued its upward trajectory, reaching 69.7% in 2023 from 57.9% in 2021. CP10 programme delivery sits within this directional improvement but the programme's contribution to MMR specifically remains attribution-difficult.
